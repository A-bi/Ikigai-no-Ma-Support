# Ikigai no Ma

**Ikigai no Ma** is a quiet, Japanese-inspired reflection app for iPhone.

It is designed as a calm digital space for Ikigai, Ma, poetry, small rituals, seasons, sound, and an inner garden. The app does not aim to be a productivity system. It is a place for slowing down, reflecting, and returning to what feels meaningful.

## Philosophy

Ikigai Garden is built around the idea that not every inner process needs to be optimized, measured, or solved immediately.

The app offers small spaces for:

* reflection without pressure
* poetic self-expression
* inner orientation
* letting go
* stillness and Ma
* seasonal awareness
* gentle ritual
* personal meaning

## Core Features

### Ikigai

Explore the four central pillars of Ikigai:

* what you love
* what you are good at
* what is needed
* what can sustain your life

### Ma

A quiet space for distance, breath, stillness, and inner spaciousness.

### Letting Go

Small rituals for acknowledging, honoring, and releasing what no longer needs to be held.

### Poetry

A calm writing space for German poems and Japanese-inspired vertical text compositions.

### Shrine

A private place for wishes, cleansing, and quiet intention.

### Seasons

Spring, summer, autumn, and winter as inner landscapes.

### Sound

Simple atmospheric soundscapes such as wind, rain, forest, bell, and silence.

### Inner Garden

A poetic garden map with interactive places such as a house, path, stone lantern, pond, bridge, shrine, tree, and interior rooms.

## Design Principles

Ikigai Garden follows a quiet design philosophy:

* less noise
* more space
* gentle interaction
* soft visual hierarchy
* Japanese-inspired aesthetics
* no unnecessary complexity
* privacy-conscious local reflection

## Privacy

Ikigai Garden is designed with privacy in mind. Personal reflections, poems, notes, and ritual entries are intended to remain personal.
