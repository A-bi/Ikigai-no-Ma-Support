# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how **Ikigai no Ma** handles personal information.

Ikigai Garden is designed as a quiet, privacy-conscious reflection app. Personal reflections, poems, notes, and ritual entries are intended to remain personal.

## Data Stored in the App

Ikigai Garden may store content locally on your device, such as:

* reflection notes
* poems
* ritual entries
* selected inner places
* preferences within the app
* saved text from app interactions

This data is used to provide the app’s core functionality.

## Local Processing

The app is designed so that reflective content can remain on the device. Personal notes, poems, and ritual entries are not intended for external tracking or advertising.

## No Tracking

Ikigai Garden does not use personal content for tracking, advertising, or user profiling.

The app is not designed to track users across apps or websites.

## Sensitive Content

The app may invite users to write personal reflections, poems, or inner notes. Please be mindful when entering sensitive personal information.

Support requests should not include private journal content, poems, health information, or other sensitive data unless it is absolutely necessary and you deliberately choose to share it.

## Third-Party Services

If future versions of the app include optional online features, such as cloud sync or external processing, these will be described clearly and will require appropriate transparency within the app and privacy documentation.

The current privacy intention of Ikigai Garden is to keep the reflective experience as private and minimal as possible.

## Support Communication

If you contact support, the information you provide may be used to understand and resolve your request. Please do not send sensitive personal content unless required for troubleshooting.

## Children

Ikigai Garden is not specifically directed at children. If the app is used by minors, it should be used with appropriate guidance from a parent or guardian.

## Medical and Therapeutic Disclaimer

Ikigai Garden is not a medical or therapeutic product. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any condition.

If you need medical, psychological, or crisis support, please contact a qualified professional or local emergency services.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the app evolves. Any changes should reflect the actual functionality and data practices of the app.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the official support contact provided with the app or on the project website.
